---
name: grilling
description: "Interview the user to stress-test a plan, idea, or decision when requested."
---

Stress-test the user's plan or decision by finding assumptions, trade-offs, and unresolved choices that affect the stated goal.

Ask a manageable round of independent questions, with a recommendation where useful. Wait for the user's answers before asking dependent questions. Look up environmental facts yourself; delegate that work when worthwhile and available. Continue independent investigation while decisions are pending.

Prioritize consequential uncertainty; do not demand exhaustive traversal of every hypothetical branch. Done when the material decisions are settled, remaining assumptions are explicit, or the user ends the interview. An interview alone does not authorize implementation, but an existing implementation request remains valid.
